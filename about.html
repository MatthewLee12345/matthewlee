About me

About Matthew.

I’m Matthew, a Columbia student, consultant, DJ, and composer based in New York.

I study Cognitive Science with a specialization in Intelligence and a minor in Computer Science at Columbia University. I’m interested in how people think, how technology works, and what happens when those two things meet.

Through Columbia Global Research and Consulting, I work with a team evaluating high-potential markets for Shake Shack’s expansion. My background spans machine-learning research, audience growth, and building community projects.

Outside of that work, I perform as b2b.mp3. I’m a resident DJ at 00:00 in Long Island City. I play sets across New York and share them with 150K+ followers across TikTok and Instagram. I also write music for film, including a first-place score at the 2024 Marvin Hamlisch International Music Awards.

I’ve researched cross-genre singing style transfer with Stanford CCRMA collaborators, founded the AI ethics nonprofit sustAIn, and brought musicians together through HeyBusking.
